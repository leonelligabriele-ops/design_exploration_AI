# Example: smartwatch data, an activity app for people over 75

**Prototypes in this folder were written by hand, following the exact prompts the
tool sends.** They were not generated by the app's AI. They show what results
from those prompts could look like. Real AI output will differ from run to run.

## The idea

> An app for people over 75 who live at home and wear a smartwatch. I want to
> encourage them to move a little every day, without making them feel judged or
> like they are failing. Many are not used to technology, so it must be very
> simple to understand. Their family or their physiotherapist may also look at it.

## The data: `watch-8-weeks.csv`

Synthetic (not a real person), 56 days of daily smartwatch summaries, made by `make-data.mjs`.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `date`, `weekday` | The day |
| `steps`, `active_minutes`, `distance_km`, `floors_climbed` | Movement |
| `outdoor_minutes` | Time outside (from GPS) |
| `sedentary_hours` | Time sitting still |
| `resting_heart_rate`, `hrv_ms` | Heart: resting rate and heart rate variability |
| `sleep_hours` | Sleep |
| `watch_worn_hours` | How long the watch was worn |

Patterns in the data:
- **Walking group:** Tuesdays and Thursdays are the most active days, with over an hour outside.
- **Quieter Sundays.**
- **An unwell week from 8 September:** about 1,100 steps a day, resting heart rate 72 instead of 66, less sleep. Recovery is slow afterwards.
- **Watch not worn on 26 September:** that row is empty.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `elderly-examples-session.json` | Open it in the tool with **Open**, then upload the CSV. It contains the cards, the exact prompts the tool recorded, the prototypes and their explanations. Made by `build-session.mjs`. |
| `elderly-examples-download.html` | The tool's **Download** file. Open it in a browser and press **Load CSV**. |
| `prototypes/` | The five prototypes as separate files. |
| `screenshots/` | Each prototype running in the tool, with its explanation. |

## The cards

| Card | Stage and function | Libraries | Idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move a little every day | Sketch · Generate | Rough.js | A very simple screen for them (yesterday's steps, the week as circles) and a longer view for family or physio. "?" boxes: how to say it kindly, which device, what a quiet day shows. |
| New representation | Explore · Explore variations | p5.js | Walking as a journey along one path: every day moves them forward, so nobody falls behind. 113 km since August; leaves mark longer walks outside. |
| New interaction | Explore · Explore variations | none (browser speech) | One big button, "How did I do yesterday?", that answers aloud and in large text, compared with their own usual days. |
| New interpretation | Explore · Explore variations | D3, simple-statistics | Each day compared with their own usual, not a target. Days with a raised resting heart rate are marked, so the quiet week reads as rest, not failure. Which weekdays help them move. |
| Move a little: app | Refine · Develop | Pico.css, simple-statistics | Near-final, large type, levels written in words as well as colour. "For me" and "Family & physio" views; the unwell week explained in one sentence. |
