# Example: one idea through Sketch, Explore and Refine

> A second example, smartwatch data for an activity app for people over 75, is in
> [elderly-activity/](elderly-activity/README.md).

**Prototypes in this folder were written by hand, following the exact prompts the
tool sends.** They were not generated by the app's AI. They show what results
from those prompts could look like. Real AI output will differ from run to run.

## The idea

> My patient is 6 weeks into rehab after ACL surgery. I want them to see how far
> their knee bends each day compared with where they started, so they stay
> motivated. I also want to notice whether pain goes up on days they exercised more.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `acl-rehab-6-weeks.csv` | Synthetic data (not a real patient): 42 days of knee bend, extension, pain, exercise minutes, steps and swelling. Two cells are empty on purpose. Made by `make-data.mjs`. |
| `acl-examples-session.json` | Open it in the tool with **Open**, then upload the CSV. It contains the cards, the exact prompts the tool recorded for each one, the prototypes and their explanations. Made by `build-session.mjs`. |
| `acl-examples-download.html` | The tool's **Download** file for this session. Open it in a browser and press **Load CSV**. |
| `prototypes/` | The five prototypes as separate files. |
| `screenshots/` | Each prototype running in the tool, with its explanation. |

## The cards

| Card | Stage and function | Libraries | Idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knee bend + pain | Sketch · Generate | Rough.js | Two hand-drawn screens: the patient's leg now vs. at the start, and the physio's pain-vs-exercise dots. Undecided parts are "?" boxes. |
| New representation | Explore · Explore variations | p5.js | Every day is one stroke of the lower leg from the same knee: six weeks open like a fan. Pain colours each stroke. |
| New interaction | Explore · Explore variations | none | Turn-taking in a session: the patient guesses their bend by dragging the leg, then the real angle is shown. The physio guesses about pain, then the data answers. |
| New interpretation | Explore · Explore variations | D3, simple-statistics | Progress against the patient's own first days, week by week. Pain compared on the same day and the day after exercise, with an honest note on the small sample. |
| Patient + physio app | Refine · Develop | GSAP, simple-statistics | Near-final app with two views: the patient's progress, and the physio's answer about pain, with the most painful days to discuss. |
